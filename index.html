Side project · formerly Mohalla · closed beta

pincode, your neighborhood

Mohalla means neighbourhood in Hindi and Urdu. pincode is a hyperlocal community app, started in Mahadevapura, Bengaluru, that shows what's happening around you, sorted by distance, on a list or a map. The web version is live.

  • Ask, Alert, Event

    Ask for or offer help, raise alerts neighbours can confirm as still happening or resolved, and share events.

  • Answers that count

    Threaded replies, "This solved it", and reputation with 9 badges.

  • Communities

    Join local groups with owners and moderators.

  • Private messages

    Message requests you can accept, decline or block, plus photos and read ticks.

  • Safety built in

    Word filter, reports, strikes and an admin area.

  • One codebase

    Android and web from the same Expo / React Native app, with fixes shipped over the air.
